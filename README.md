# Honda OBD2 Diagnostics - Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Honda OBD2 Diagnostics does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

Vehicle and diagnostic data received from a compatible ELM327 adapter is processed locally on the user's device. The app does not upload this diagnostic data to a developer-operated server.

The app may request Bluetooth, nearby devices, location, Wi-Fi, and internet permissions only to discover and connect to compatible OBD2 adapters, communicate with the vehicle, and use Google Play services. Android may require location permission for Bluetooth discovery on some OS versions. The app does not use this permission to track or store the user's location.

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment information is handled by Google and is not received or stored by the developer.

Reports are exported or shared only when the user explicitly chooses an export or share action. Data stored locally by the app can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

This app is intended for OBD2/EOBD-readable Honda vehicles. OBD1 vehicles, including Honda CR-V RD1 configurations that require an OBD1-to-OBD2 converter, are outside this app's scope.
